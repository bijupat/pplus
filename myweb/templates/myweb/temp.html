Your health is our priority, and we use the latest equipment and tools for accurate results. We are one of the best labs in Ahmedabad offering error-free reports for over 2 decades. Our stringent quality control checks and capable lab technicians ensure the most accurate testing results. Moreover, we use bar-coded instruments to generate computerized reports.

We take pride in stating that Shubham Pathology Laboratory is a fully automated, modern lab. We have some of the best lab equipment, such as Automated Cell Counter, Semi-Automated Blood Chemistry Analyser and Computerised Blood Testing.

Using advanced technology in our Pathology Laboratory in Ahmedabad, we provide a wide range of Diagnostic testing services. We provide timely reports so you need not visit us several times. We can also deliver your reports via SMS or email. You can even book an appointment with us for home sample collection.

Since we carry-out all routine lab tests under one roof, you do not need to visit any other lab for your health check-up. We are located centrally and are also open on holidays.
